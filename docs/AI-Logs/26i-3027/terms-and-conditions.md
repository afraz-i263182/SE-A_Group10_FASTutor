## 1. Objective

Develop a centralized legal and policy page for FASTutor where students and tutors can review platform terms, privacy information, and community expectations.

## 2. Prompt / Task Description

Develop a Terms & Conditions page for the FASTutor tutoring platform. Maintain consistency with the existing website's design system.

The page should have three navigable sections or tabs:
1. Terms of Use
2. Privacy Policy
3. Community Guidelines

The Terms of Use should cover:
- Student and tutor responsibilities
- Appropriate platform usage
- Account conduct
- Session cancellations
- Refund-related guidelines

The Privacy Policy should cover:
- Information collected from users
- How information is used
- Data storage and protection
- Data sharing
- User privacy considerations

The Community Guidelines should cover:
- Respectful communication
- Academic integrity
- Prohibited behavior
- Appropriate tutor/student interactions
- Reporting concerns

Include a last-updated date and ensure the page is responsive.

## 3. Implementation Approach

- Use a tabbed interface to organize policy content.
- Follow FASTutor's typography, colors, and card styling.
- Use headings and content sections to improve readability.
- Ensure tabs can be navigated easily.
- Keep styling isolated from unrelated website components.
- Provide responsive layouts for mobile and desktop.
