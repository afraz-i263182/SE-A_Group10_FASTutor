How FASTutor works

Whether you're stuck on a course or ready to teach one, it takes three steps.

  1. 01

    Find your match

    Search by course code, campus and rating. Every profile shows the grade the senior earned in that exact course, so you pick someone who has already done what you're about to do.

    searchFilter by campus and course
  2. 02

    Book a session

    Choose in-person or online, pick a time slot that fits your timetable, and pay by online transfer or cash on campus. Confirmation goes to your NU email.

    Ace the exam

Our story & mission

Engineered by students to help juniors master tough subjects and labs.

Sessional weeks arrive all at once and the syllabus is long. The person best placed to help is usually a senior who sat the same paper a year or two ago, but a junior rarely knows who that is, or whether their grade backs it up.

FASTutor is that introduction. Seniors prove their results with their Flex transcript, juniors search by course code, and both meet on campus or online at a student-sized price. Our tutors know the lab tasks and paper patterns because they have been through them.

3-step tutor verification

Every tutor clears all three checks before their profile goes live.

  • Flex transcript verified

    An A or A- in the course they teach, shown on their official Flex portal transcript.

  • Minimum 3.5 GPA

    Strong results overall, not just one good course.

  • Official NU email

    Linked to an active nu.edu.pk address, so every tutor is a real FASTian.
