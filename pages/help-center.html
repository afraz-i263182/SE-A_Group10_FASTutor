Campus Safety & Academic Integrity

Four habits that keep every session safe and fair for everyone.

  • location_on

    Meet in Public Spots

    Choose busy campus areas such as the library, labs or study corners for in-person sessions.

  • verified_user

    Verify @nu Identity

    Look for the Verified badge on your tutor's profile, and use your own official @nu.edu.pk email to sign up.

  • gavel

    Uphold FAST Honor Code

    Sessions are for learning. Tutors explain concepts and review past papers; they never complete graded work for you.

    Save your transfer receipt or booking confirmation. It is the first thing support will ask for if there is a payment issue.

Quick answers about verification, sessions and payments.

Groups

Join an active Exam Rush cohort or book a group slot with 3–5 batchmates. A verified senior runs the session and the hourly fee is shared between everyone.

Splitting the hourly fee typically works out to PKR 250–350 per student, which makes intensive past-paper reviews much cheaper than a one-on-one.

Group sessions run on campus or online. The mode is shown when you book, so you know where to be before you pay.

Exam Rush

Exam Rush is emergency revision run by verified standby seniors from 8:00 PM to 2:00 AM on the nights before Sessional 1, Sessional 2 and Finals.

Open the Exam Rush section on the home page, pick a session for your course and reserve your spot.

Bring your course outline, past papers and a list of the topics you find hardest, so the senior can spend the night on what you actually need.
