Terms of Use

Using FASTutor means agreeing to these basic rules.

1. About FASTutor

FASTutor is a student-focused peer mentorship concept intended to help FAST-NUCES students connect for academic support. It is not an official FAST-NUCES service or administration platform.

2. Student responsibilities

  • Provide accurate information when creating a profile or requesting a session.
  • Use tutoring for learning and practice, not to obtain answers for graded work dishonestly.
  • Communicate respectfully and arrive prepared for booked sessions.
  • Do not share another person's account, contact details, or private materials without permission.

3. Tutor responsibilities

  • Represent your academic experience, grades, and skills honestly.
  • Explain concepts and encourage independent learning rather than completing assignments on a student's behalf.
  • Honor agreed session times, rates, and format; communicate changes promptly.
  • Keep student information and session conversations confidential.

4. Sessions, cancellations & refunds

Students and tutors should confirm the session time, format, duration, and fee before a booking. If a cancellation is needed, notify the other person as early as possible. Any refund or credit should be agreed and handled through the project's stated booking/payment process. This prototype does not process or guarantee payments or refunds.

5. Academic integrity & prohibited use

FASTutor must not be used for plagiarism, cheating during examinations, impersonation, harassment, discrimination, fraud, or sharing restricted academic materials. Users may be removed from the service concept for misconduct.

6. Limitations

Peer tutoring is not a guarantee of grades, exam outcomes, course completion, or tutor availability. Users are responsible for evaluating whether a tutor's advice suits their needs.

Privacy Policy

Respecting the information students choose to share.

1. Information we may collect

A real FASTutor service could collect account details (such as name and university email), campus and course preferences, tutoring requests, session details, feedback, and messages submitted through the platform.

2. How information may be used

  • To create and manage student or tutor profiles.
  • To connect students with relevant tutors and coordinate sessions.
  • To support safety, resolve disputes, and improve the service.
  • To communicate important booking or service updates.

3. Sharing & visibility

Profile details needed to arrange tutoring may be visible to other users involved in a session. Personal information should not be sold. It may only be shared with service providers needed to operate a real service or where legally required, with appropriate safeguards.

4. Data security & retention

A live service should use reasonable security controls, limit access to personal data, and retain information only for as long as necessary for its purpose. No online service can promise absolute security.

5. Your choices

Users should be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information and withdraw from the service. Contact the project team through the channels provided by the live platform for privacy requests.

6. Prototype notice

This student project page is informational. It does not describe a verified production data system, and this prototype itself does not submit information to a backend from this page.

Community Guidelines

Keep the peer-learning space helpful, safe, and welcoming.

Be respectful

Speak to classmates and tutors with courtesy. Personal attacks, bullying, discriminatory remarks, intimidation, and unwanted conduct are not acceptable.

Keep sessions academic

Ask questions, work through examples, share legitimate resources, and help others understand. Do not use tutoring to cheat, plagiarize, or bypass course rules.

Honor commitments

Be punctual, let others know if plans change, and agree on session expectations in advance. Do not pressure someone into extra sessions, payments, or sharing private details.

Protect privacy

Do not post screenshots of chats, student IDs, grades, phone numbers, or personal information without consent. Report suspicious or inappropriate behavior to the project team.

Consequences

Repeated or serious violations may result in warnings, restrictions, removal of content, or suspension from the service concept, depending on the situation.
